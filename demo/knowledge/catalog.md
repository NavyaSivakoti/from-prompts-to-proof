and hiking. It does not sell fashion dresses or formalwear. Its complete demo
catalog consists of the five products below.

All prices are in U.S. dollars. Availability is fixed fictional demo data,
not live inventory. The assistant can describe these listed statuses but cannot
check current stock, reserve or purchase an item, or give a restock date. There
is no actual Northwind shopping website or checkout in this demonstration.

## Limits of the supplied information

The assistant can compare the descriptions, listed prices, sizes, colors, and
demo availability above. It must not invent additional products, colors, sizes,
materials, specifications, measurements, fit guarantees, or restock dates.
No size chart or body measurements are supplied. No waterproof rating is
supplied for the backpack, boots, or tent.

An offered option is part of the catalog; it is not necessarily available.
For example, Navy M is an offered Summit Rain Jacket option but is sold out.
Use the status of the exact product, color, and size, rather than treating all
options for a product as available. A sold-out variant cannot be selected as
an available purchase in the demo data. It has no supplied restock date.

General product selection, sizing limits, and fictional care guidance are
provided separately in the product-help document. Shopping and order guidance
explain the fictional purchase process; they do not create a working checkout.

# Product Selection, Sizing, and Care

These are fictional Northwind Outfitters product-help guidelines for this
demonstration. Northwind is an outdoor clothing and gear store, not a fashion
or formalwear store. It does not sell dresses. Its entire catalog contains
the Summit Rain Jacket, Trail Fleece, Daypack20 Backpack, Ridge Hiking Boots,
and Pine Two-Person Tent. Use the catalog for their exact prices, options, and
fixed demo availability.

## Choosing and comparing products

Ask what the customer needs the item for, their budget, and any relevant size
or color preference when those details are missing. A suggestion can be based
on the supplied descriptions; it cannot guarantee suitability, fit, or
performance. The assistant can compare catalog facts without making a purchase.

- Summit Rain Jacket: an outdoor jacket with water resistance. It is not
  waterproof. It may be considered for an outing with light rain, but the
  supplied information does not guarantee that a wearer will stay dry.
- Trail Fleece: a warm outdoor layer. It is not waterproof. It can be considered
  when warmth is the customer's stated priority. No temperature rating is
  supplied, so do not promise comfort in a particular temperature.
- Daypack20 Backpack: a backpack with a 20-liter capacity. It can be considered
  for carrying items on an outdoor outing. No load limit, compartment layout,
  dimensions, or waterproof performance is supplied.
- Ridge Hiking Boots: outdoor hiking boots. No terrain rating, traction test,
  insulation rating, or waterproof performance is supplied.
- Pine Two-Person Tent: the listed model accommodates two people and is sold
  out in the fixed demo data. Do not present it as an available purchase. No
  season rating, weather rating, weight, or dimensions are supplied.

There is no waterproof item confirmed by this knowledge base: the jacket and
fleece are explicitly not waterproof, while waterproof information for the
backpack, boots, and tent is unknown. Unknown performance is not a guarantee
and is not evidence that a product definitely lacks that feature.

For a budget comparison, use the listed item price and exact available variant.
For multiple items or quantities, sum the listed merchandise prices and apply
any supplied promotion rules before comparing the result with a budget or the
shipping threshold. Tax, shipping, and any applicable return cost are separate
from the merchandise subtotal. Do not invent discounts, package deals, or
additional products. A budget that includes all charges needs those charges to
be considered; an unknown tax amount prevents an exact final checkout total.

A birthday or gift does not change the catalog. Outdoor clothing or gear can
be discussed for a recipient who wants those items, but the assistant cannot
guarantee the recipient's preferences or fit. It cannot supply a dress,
formalwear, or an unlisted product. No gift-wrapping or gift-receipt policy is
supplied, so do not promise those options.

## Sizing and colors

S, M, L, and XL mean small, medium, large, and extra large. They are the offered
size labels for the jacket and fleece, not body-measurement recommendations.
There is no size chart, garment measurement table, or fit guarantee. Height,
weight, age, or a size from another brand alone cannot establish which size fits.

The boots use whole U.S. size labels 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11, with no half sizes.
No men's/women's category, width options, international conversion chart, or
fit measurements are supplied. Do not turn a listed size into a fit guarantee.
The backpack is one size. The tent is a two-person model, not a clothing size.

Match the exact requested size and color to the catalog's fixed status. An
offered size can still be sold out in one color and available in another.
Color names describe the listed options; no photographs or exact color swatches
are supplied. The assistant cannot promise the appearance of an unlisted color
or create a special size, color, or custom product.

## Fictional care guidance

These care steps are explicitly defined demo guidance, not claims about a
product's material composition. Follow an item's supplied care label if it
contains more specific instructions. No material composition is supplied.

- Summit Rain Jacket: use a cold, gentle wash; avoid bleach and fabric softener;
  let it air dry. No reproofing product or treatment is specified.
- Trail Fleece: use a cold, gentle wash, avoid bleach, and let it air dry.
- Daypack20 Backpack: empty it, remove loose dirt, and wipe it with a damp cloth
  and mild soap. Let it air dry. Do not machine wash it.
- Ridge Hiking Boots: remove loose dirt with a soft brush or damp cloth, then
  let them air dry away from direct heat. Do not machine wash them. No polish,
  conditioner, or waterproofing treatment is specified.
- Pine Two-Person Tent: remove loose dirt, wipe it with a damp cloth, and allow
  it to dry completely before packing or storage. Do not machine wash it.

Care guidance does not create a warranty or coverage for later damage. If an
item arrived damaged, use the supplied damaged-item support process. The
assistant cannot inspect the item or promise that a cleaning step fixes damage.

## Availability and assistance limits

Product availability is fixed fictional demo data, not live stock. The assistant
cannot reserve an item, place a backorder, arrange a custom order, sign someone
up for restock notifications, supply a restock date, or contact support for them.
It may describe a listed available alternative while explaining any different
size, color, price, or product purpose. It must not treat an alternative as the
same product or promise that it meets an unsupported requirement.

# Human Support, Contact, and Privacy Boundaries

Northwind Outfitters and its support contact are fictional demo information.
Human support is listed at support@northwind.example.
Support hours are Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Arizona time.
The expected response time is 1–2 business days; this is an expectation,
not a guaranteed deadline or proof that an individual request was received.

Northwind has no physical store, in-person pickup, or telephone support hotline
in this demonstration. Do not invent a phone number, street address, store
location, pickup service, or extra contact channel.

## Contacting human support

Tell the customer to contact human support directly for an individual request.
Where the relevant policy calls for an order number, item, reason, or damage
photos, those details should go directly to human support, not into this chat.
Do not say that the assistant has sent an email, contacted support, opened a
ticket, escalated a case, checked an order, or completed a requested action.
The assistant has no tool that performs any of those actions.

## Sensitive information

Do not request or encourage sharing full card numbers, card security codes/CVV,
card PINs, one-time passcodes/OTPs, account passwords, or API keys in this chat.
If the customer offers those details, tell them not to share them here and
provide a practical next step without requesting or repeating the credentials.
For payment failures, the customer can check billing information privately and
contact their bank or human support themselves.

No complete privacy, data-retention, or regulatory-compliance policy is supplied.
Do not invent a retention period, claim compliance with a particular framework,
promise that nothing is retained, or assert technical privacy guarantees.
The assistant can explain these handling boundaries without claiming that a
privacy or security audit has been completed.

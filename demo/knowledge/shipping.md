# Shipping

These are fictional Northwind Outfitters policies for this demonstration.

Northwind Outfitters ships to the contiguous 48 United States only.
It does not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, or international destinations.

Order processing normally takes 1–2 business days before dispatch.
Standard shipping normally takes 3–5 business days after dispatch.
Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. Processing and
transit are separate estimates: neither is a guaranteed delivery date. A birthday,
trip, or other deadline does not change these estimates. The assistant cannot
promise next-day arrival or confirm an individual order's dispatch date.
Standard shipping costs $5.99 for orders below $75 and is free for orders of $75
or more. The threshold uses the merchandise subtotal after discounts and before
tax. This standard rate applies throughout the supported shipping area; a ZIP
code alone does not change it.

Expedited shipping may be available during checkout.
Expedited shipping has a separate charge and does not qualify for the free
standard shipping offer.
The checkout page shows available options, expedited prices, and delivery
estimates for an order. Free standard shipping does not mean free expedited
shipping. No expedited rate or guaranteed delivery time is supplied here.

The fictional store accepts street delivery addresses in the supported area.
P.O. boxes, APO/FPO addresses, and store pickup are not supported. The assistant
can explain these destination rules but cannot validate an individual address.

If tracking says delivered but the parcel is missing, check the delivery area
and with household members, then contact human support with the order details
directly. Human support reviews the shipment; no automatic replacement or refund
is promised. If an estimated delivery period passes, contact human support
rather than assuming the parcel is lost. The assistant cannot contact a carrier.

The assistant can explain these rates, but cannot inspect a cart, quote an
individual expedited order, validate an address, or check a shipment itself.

An order confirmation is emailed after checkout. If it is missing, check spam
and junk folders, search your inbox for Northwind Outfitters, and verify that
the email address entered at checkout was correct. Tracking information is
emailed when the order is dispatched; it may not be available before dispatch.

If an email is still missing or an individual shipment needs attention,
contact human support at support@northwind.example, a fictional contact.
The assistant cannot look up an order, check its status, or resend an email.
Do not ask for a customer's phone number or email address in this chat.
Any necessary order information should go directly to human support.

# Returns

These are fictional Northwind Outfitters policies for this demonstration.

## Standard return eligibility

Standard products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be unused and in original condition.
The window starts at delivery, not the purchase or order date.

## How to request a standard return

1. Check that the item meets the return window and condition requirements.
2. Email human support at support@northwind.example with the order number,
   item, and reason for the return. This contact is fictional. Send these order
   details directly to human support, rather than sharing them in this chat.
3. Wait for support to review the request and send return instructions and a
   prepaid return label. Do not invent a return address or send an item before
   receiving instructions.
4. Pack the item securely and use the supplied label and instructions.
5. Support receives and inspects the item before deciding whether to approve
   the refund. The assistant cannot approve a return or issue a label.

## Return costs

For an ordinary standard return, the prepaid return label costs $5.99, deducted
from the approved refund. There is no restocking fee.
The original shipping charge is not refunded for an ordinary return.

For example, an approved ordinary return of one $45 Daypack20 Backpack has a
$39.01 merchandise refund after the $5.99 label deduction. Any original shipping
charge remains non-refundable. This example excludes tax; human support handles
the final refund and any paid tax. The assistant cannot inspect an actual refund.

## Size or color changes

The fictional store does not offer direct ordinary exchanges. For a different
size or color, request an eligible standard return through human support and
make a separate purchase of a listed available replacement. That replacement is
not reserved or guaranteed. The assistant cannot place either request or order.
Replacement after confirmed arrival damage is a separate human-reviewed process.

Used or worn products are not eligible for an ordinary standard return. Damage
on arrival follows the damaged-item process below; do not apply the unused-item
condition to reject arrival damage before human review.

## Damaged items

If the problem is unclear, first ask which product is affected, what is wrong,
and whether it arrived damaged or was damaged later. Do not assume the product,
cause, timing, or resolution from a message such as "It's broken."

If an item arrived damaged, ask the customer to contact human support with the
order number, item, a description of the damage, and photos of the item and
packaging. Keep the item and packaging until support provides instructions.
These details and photos should be sent directly to human support.

After human support confirms damage on arrival, it provides a prepaid return
label at no return-label charge if a return is needed. Support determines
whether to offer a replacement, subject to availability, or an approved refund.
Do not promise a replacement or refund before review, treat later damage as
confirmed arrival damage, or claim the assistant has arranged a resolution.
No coverage or specific resolution for damage occurring later is supplied here.

## Refund timing and method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
Refund processing may take 5–7 business days after approval, not after the
initial request or the date an item is posted back.
The assistant cannot process a refund, check a payment record, send a
confirmation email, or contact support for the customer.
